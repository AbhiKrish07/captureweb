Trust & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Quiet Studio

1. Our Privacy Commitments

Quiet Studio builds Capture as a desktop-first personal context workspace. We believe in strict boundaries and clear data ownership. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we run background advertisements.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information solely to provide, authenticate, and improve Capture:

  • Account Information: Your email address and verification credentials used during authentication.
  • Captured Content & Space Metadata: Files, notes, links, and documents you explicitly capture and place in Spaces. Content is stored locally on your device and synchronized to secure cloud infrastructure for backup and remote access.
  • Application Telemetry: Anonymized diagnostic logs (crash reports, version checks, and basic performance benchmarks) to ensure client stability.

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Providers

To power agent capabilities (such as arranging documents, generating syntheses, or semantic clustering), Capture transmits permitted context to frontier AI API providers (such as Anthropic and OpenAI) under enterprise commercial agreements that guarantee:

  • Customer data submitted via API endpoints is never used to train base foundation models.
  • Zero retention or transient ephemeral retention policies apply as mandated by enterprise API service terms.
  • Data transmissions occur exclusively over authenticated TLS 1.3 encryption.

4. Local Context & Model Context Protocol (MCP)

When you run Capture's local MCP server for Claude Desktop or Cursor, communications occur locally on your machine via stdio. Capture only provides context for Spaces that you have explicitly toggled as accessible to that tool. You can revoke access or terminate the MCP daemon at any moment.

5. User Control, Data Export & Deletion

You maintain total sovereignty over your data:

  • Full Data Export: You can export all your Spaces, files, notes, and agent action logs in open formats (Markdown, JSON, original raw attachments) at any time.
  • Account & Space Deletion: If you delete a Space or close your account, all associated document vectors and cloud database records are permanently purged from active servers within 30 days.

6. Contact & Inquiries

For privacy questions or data subject access requests, reach our privacy team at privacy@captureqs.space.
