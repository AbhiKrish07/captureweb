Structured Workspaces

Your projects deserve more than folders.

Spaces bring all the information surrounding a project into one structured working environment. Instead of nested folder mazes, organize by spatial context and semantic relationships.

01

Everything in one place

Documents, images, links, notes and screenshots live side by side. A single Space holds the technical architecture PDF, the customer interview quotes, the Figma design references, and the terminal logs.

02

Organize spatially

Drag items into functional clusters. Create logical zones for specifications, competitive teardowns, and sprint deliverables without creating nesting mazes.

03

Let the agent organize

Capture understands document semantic relationships. As you drop items into a Space, the agent suggests natural groupings and clusters based on content.

04

Your Space evolves with your work

New captures can be added incrementally over weeks or months. Existing context stays preserved, referenced and queryable without needing migration.

05

You remain in control

Review all agent actions in an explicit linear timeline. Accept suggested structures, manually rearrange cards, or instantly undo automated operations.
