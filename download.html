Step-by-Step

macOS Installation Guide

  1. Download Capture: Receive your private beta invitation link containing the DMG installer.
  2. Open the Installer: Double-click Capture-0.1.0-Universal.dmg.
  3. Move to Applications: Drag the Capture app icon into your macOS Applications folder.
  4. Launch & Sign In: Open Capture and sign in with your approved beta email.
  5. Configure Shortcut: Choose your global shortcut (defaults to ⌥ Space).
  6. Create Your First Space: Hit ⌘N to build a project Space and start dragging context.

Troubleshooting & Permissions

To enable system-wide screen capture and clipboard observation on macOS, ensure the following permissions are granted under System Settings > Privacy & Security:

  • Accessibility: Needed to register the global ⌥ Space shortcut when other apps are active.
  • Screen Recording: Required strictly for the regional screen capture snipping tool. No continuous recording takes place.
