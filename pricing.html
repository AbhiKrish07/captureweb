Future Roadmap

Our pricing philosophy

We do not publish artificial prices before actual model usage costs are validated. Below is the working hypothesis:

Free

Personal users

Intended for individual knowledge storage, standard captures, and personal Spaces with local indexing and essential agent capabilities.

Pro

Individual builders & developers

Higher AI reasoning capacity, expanded context window processing, full MCP bridges to Claude & Cursor, and automated contradiction checks.

Power

Advanced long-term memory synthesis, multi-Space cross-referencing, self-hosted LLM endpoints, and prioritized processing queues.
