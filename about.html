Quiet Studio

Building a better relationship between information and work.

Capture is being built by Quiet Studio. We craft desktop-first tools that respect human attention, preserve intellectual context, and elevate personal agency.

Information has become infinitely easier to collect, but not necessarily easier to use.

Files, links, screenshots, research and conversations accumulate across applications. AI tools can process individual pieces of information, but they almost always lack the persistent context needed to understand the larger project.

Every time you switch between your browser, terminal, PDF reader, and an AI chat window, you have to mentally rebuild that context from scratch.

Capture is our attempt to solve that problem. We believe the next generation of personal software should organize information around what people are actually working on, not just where they save it.
Principles

How we design software

Desktop-first craftsmanship

Web apps are convenient, but desktop apps own keyboard latency, system-wide shortcuts, and offline reliability. We build native experiences for the Mac.

Grounded, verifiable AI

Every factual claim, summary, and suggestion made by an agent should trace directly back to the original source document. We reject ungrounded hallucinations.

Sovereign data & open standards

Your context should never be trapped in a proprietary walled garden. We build around the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and ensure full local exportability.

Restrained, editorial calm

No gamification, flashing badges, or neon gradients. Software should feel like high-grade stationery: quiet, dependable, and durable.
