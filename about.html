Quiet Studio

Building a better relationship between information and work.

Capture is being built by Quiet Studio. We craft desktop-first personal computing tools that preserve intellectual context and elevate human agency.

Information has become exponentially easier to collect, but not necessarily easier to use.

Files, links, screenshots, research and conversations accumulate across applications. AI tools can process individual snippets of data, but they lack the persistent, ambient context needed to understand the true scope of a project.

Every time you switch between your editor, browser tabs, PDF reader, and AI chats, you have to mentally rebuild that context from scratch.

Capture is our attempt to solve that problem. We believe the next generation of personal software should organize information around what people are actually working on, not just where they save it.
Our Craft

Studio Principles

Desktop-first craftsmanship

Web apps are convenient, but native desktop clients own shortcut speed, clipboard integration, and offline reliability. We build natively for macOS.

Grounded, verifiable AI

Every summary, suggestion, and citation connects directly back to an authentic file inside your Space. We reject ungrounded hallucinations.

Sovereign data & open standards

Your context should never be trapped in a proprietary lock-in silo. We build around the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and provide full open exports.

Restrained, editorial calm

No gamification or flashing neon distractions. Software should feel like dependable stationery: durable, quiet, and timeless.
